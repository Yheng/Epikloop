# Screens

## cart Cart
- States: default, empty, loading, error
- Requirements: REQ-CHECKOUT-002

Each item in the cart with its price, and the total with tax as its own line. A person reaches it
from the cart button on every page.

## sign-in Sign in
- States: default, error
- Requirements: REQ-CHECKOUT-001

Shown before payment to anyone who isn't signed in, who returns to the cart afterwards.
