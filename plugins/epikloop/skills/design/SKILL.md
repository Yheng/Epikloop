---
description: Design every screen of the product, from three direction variants the human chooses between to tokens, a manifest and a gallery.
argument-hint: '[amend <screen> "<change>" | add <screen> | audit | ingest]'
effort: high
---

Outcome: the product's visual truth lives in `design/`. A design brief, drawing on any images in
`design/refs/`, leads to three direction variants of the system sheet as HTML; the human picks one,
and every screen follows at both widths, in four states, with real copy, alongside `tokens.css`,
`manifest.md` and a `design/index.html` gallery. The sub-modes change one thing at a time: `amend`
a screen, `add` a screen, `audit` the set for gaps, or `ingest` a screen designed elsewhere.
Scope: lanes with a UI; the service lane skips design.
