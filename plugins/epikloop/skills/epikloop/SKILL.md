---
name: epikloop
description: Show where this project stands, what needs the human and the one next step, and start that step when the human says go.
effort: low
---

Outcome: in a few lines the human knows where the project stands and what to do next, and the next
step starts when they say "go". The stage comes from the stage machine's mechanical done-tests, and
the next step is the first test that fails, so the answer is the same after lunch or after six
weeks. The report opens with the fuel gauge, `MVP done/total · velocity · ETA · queue Δ`, calling
the ETA fiction out loud when the queue delta is positive, then names what needs the human and one
recommended command. Outside an Epikloop project it offers `discover` for a new product or `adopt`
for existing code. Scope: nothing changes beyond syncing with the remote; the work belongs to the
skill it starts.
